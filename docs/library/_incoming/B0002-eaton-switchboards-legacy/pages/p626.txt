10   Count controls are counters
     that provide output signal(s)
                                            Application Example

     at preset count value(s).                        Rotary Contactor
11   Typical applications include
     cut-to-length, batching, filling,
     mixing and dispensing. A
12   variety of count controls in
     different sizes, display types
     and feature sets are available
13   from Eaton.
