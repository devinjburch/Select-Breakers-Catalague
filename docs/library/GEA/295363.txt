Turn OFF power ahead of the panelboard or switchboard before working inside the equipment or removing any component . Do not remove circuit protective devices or any other component until the power is turned OFF. General These instructions apply to the following catalog numbers: • Circuit breaker modules AMC6JK, AMC4JK, ACM3JK and AMC2JK • Circuit breaker frames TJK, TJD, TJJ, THJK, TJ4V, THJ4V, and, TJL4V. Installation 1. Phase balancing for two-pole devices in three-phase systems. To balance the panelboard load, remove the screws on the appropriate bus clip, reposition the bus clip as shown in Figure 1, then install and tighten the screws to 27–32 in-lb. Figure 1. Repositioning the bus clip to balance the load. 2. Prepare the breaker module. Loosen the latch lock screws and fully retract the rail latches. Line up the guide fingers on both ends of the module with the notches in the panelboard interior rails, as shown in Figure 2. Figure 2. Preparing the circuit breaker module for installation. 3. Install the module. Latch one side of the circuit breaker module. Release the rail latch. Pivot the module onto the bus bars and engage the second latch. Release the rail latch. Tighten the rail latch screws to 25 in-lb as shown in Figure 3. Figure 3. Installing the circuit breaker module.  Advertising 4. Install the circuit breaker on the module. a. For main devices (lugs only on ON side of circuit breaker). Place the OFF side of the circuit breaker over the stud posts, as shown in Figure 4, with the formed barriers on the module underneath the breaker. Secure the breaker to the module with round-head screws and lock washers tightened to 35–50 in.-lb. and to the stud posts with nut-washer assemblies tightened to 80–110 in-lb. Figure 4. Installing a main breaker. b. For center-mounted branch circuit breakers (lugs on OFF side of breaker). Place the ON side of the circuit breaker over the stud posts, as shown in Figure 5, with the formed barriers on the module underneath the breaker. Secure the breaker to the module with round-head screws and lock washers tightened to 35– 50 in.-lb. and to the stud posts with nut-washer assemblies tightened to 80–110 in-lb. Figure 5. Installing a center-mounted branch breaker. c. For double-mounted branch circuit breakers. Place the ON side of each circuit breaker over the stud posts, as shown in Figure 6, with the formed barriers on the module to the outside of the breakers. Secure the load ends with round-head screws and lock washers tightened to 35–50 in.-lb. Place the straps across the line terminals of opposing breakers and secure with nut-washer assemblies tightened to 80– 110 in-lb. Combinations of TJK, TJD, TJJ, and THJK breakers may be used and combinations of TJ4V, THJ4V, and TJL4V breakers may be used. Figure 6. Installing a double-mounted branch breaker. d. For one circuit breaker mounted on a double-branch module. Place the ON side of the circuit breaker over the stud posts, as shown in Figure 7, with the formed barriers on the module to the outside of the breaker. Secure the load end with round-head screws and lock washers tightened to 35–50 in.-lb. Place one spacer over each of the unused stud posts. Place one strap across each breaker line terminal and the opposite spacer and secure with nut-washer assemblies tightened to 80–110 in-lb. Figure 7. Installing one breaker on a double-branch module.  Advertising 5. Wire the circuits. Refer to the label on the circuit breaker for the proper tightening torque. 6. Filler plate kits. Install filler plate kit AFP6TJS with AMC2JK and AMC3JK modules. Install filler plate kit AFP6TJD with AMC4JK and AMC6JK modules. Procedure for Aluminum Terminations 1. Strip the insulation, being careful to not nick the wire. 2. Clean the wire strands with a wire brush. 3. Thoroughly coat the stripped conductor with a suitable antioxidant compound, such as ALNOX or PENETROX A13. 4. Insert the conductor and tighten the connector screw to the torque indicated on the rating label.
