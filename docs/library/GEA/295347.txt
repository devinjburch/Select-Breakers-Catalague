Marker strips to use for various interior heights. Drill the Mounting Holes WARNING: Eye protection is required for this operation. Use a marker as a template. Place the strip at the dimension shown in Figure 1 (from the outside edge of the side trim) and directly opposite the mounted devices. Drill a 0.120-inch diameter hole (#31 drill) at each end of the marker. Some markers will have more than two holes, depending on their lengths. Install the Marker Strips Insert a marker into the retainer strips mounting holes next to the circuit to be identified. Form the marker legs over to secure the marker in the mounting strip hole. When all the required circuit markers are attached, install the retainer strip to the front shield with the #6-32 pan- head thread-forming screws provided, as shown in Figure 2. Figure 1.
