Do not remove circuit protective devices or any other component until the power is turned OFF. General These instructions apply to the following circuit breaker frames: SGDA, SGHA, SGLA, and SGPA. Install the space filler. A space filler is only required with a single breaker installed in a double-branch configuration. Secure the steel space filler with two #10-32 x 3 / 8 " screws, tightened to 25 in-lb, as shown in Figures 1 and 2. 4. Install the cover plate and center barrier. • Single-branch applications. Attach the plastic spacer bars, as shown in 3, with four #6-20 x 1" washer-head screws and four #6 nut clips. Attach the cover plate assembly to the circuit breaker. Tighten the screws to 10 in-lb. • Double-branch applications. Assemble two #10-16 x 1 / 2 " washer-head screws and two #10 nut clips in the cover, as shown in Figure 1 for a single breaker and Figure 4 for two breakers.
