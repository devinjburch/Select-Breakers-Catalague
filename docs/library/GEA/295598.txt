 Advertising g Spectra Series™ Power Panelboards Equipment Grounds, Types AEG 10, 21, 21S, 31S Installation WARNING: Danger of electrical shock or injury. Turn OFF power ahead of the panelboard or switchboard before working inside the equipment or removing any component . Equipment is to be installed and maintained by properly trained and qualified personnel only. See Figures 1 and 2 for illustrations of AEG10 and AEG21 uninsulated equipment grounds and Figures 3 and 4 for illustrations of AEG21S and AEG31S insulated equipment grounds. 1. Attach terminals to the straps as shown in the appropriate figure. Find the holes for mounting the equipment ground at the opposite end of the box from where it is stamped Line . If the box is painted, scratch through the paint in the area where the strap is to contact the box. Mount the equipment ground with the two 10-32 thread-forming screws provided and tighten to 25-30 in-lb. 2. Remove the paper backing from the identification label provided. Place the label below the equipment ground, approximately in the position shown. Figure 1. Equipment ground catalog number AEG10. Figure 2. Equipment ground catalog number AEG21. Figure 3.
