Constructed numbers: Square D LA1( ) field-installable accessories built from OEM rule (LA1 + Table 3.60 suffix) carry conf B except OEM-printed LA11212 and LA1G. Hold-down/retainer kits found only for QO load centers (PK2MB, PK3MB, PK5RK, PK4MB2LA/HA, QO2DTIM) and Siemens EQ/Ultimate load centers (ECMBR1/2); none found for panelboard back-fed mains (see leads). Mounting hardware for surface mounting (Eaton BMH, Siemens BQ face plates) flagged in questions as not panelboard-interior hardware.
