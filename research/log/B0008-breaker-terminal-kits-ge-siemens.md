## Narrative

Downloaded GE BuyLog Section 6 Rev 1/19 (mirror), Section 6 Rev 11/13, Section 3 Rev 9/12, a 2008 Section 3 excerpt and a single 2013 lug page. Downloaded Siemens SPEEDFAX 2017 Section 7 (Coburn's mirror; the Siemens support URL returned an HTML stub) and the 2021 Section 7 revision (IPS mirror). Extracted with pdftotext -layout. GE source tables: p. 6-102 (general lugs, copper lugs, line shields, TKM lug covers), 6-103 (Spectra lugs, covers, LV kits), 6-104 (PD lugs), 6-36/37 (TJJ/TJK lug columns), 6-48 (SG lug kits), 6-73/78/81/88 (Record Plus) and 6-101 (back-connected studs). Footnote superscripts are fused into part numbers in the GE extraction. Base numbers were resolved by comparing the 2012 and 2019 footnote numbering, and the as-printed variants are recorded as alias alt_part_numbers. Prior TCAL numbers (TCAL812, TCAL1214, etc.) are print artifacts, not supersessions. Genuine table changes 2012 to 2013/2019: TCAL27, TCALK341, TCALK343, TCALK347, TJLVK, 789A448G3(?) and 286A8066G1 were dropped. Siemens tables used: 7-39/7-40 (BQ/QR2), ED/FD/JD/MD/PD breaker pages, 7-153/154/155 (lug master tables), 7-160 (terminal shields) and 7-165 (rear-connecting studs). Not found: GE TCAL28/38/39/51/62, Siemens TA1D/TA1F/TA1G/TA2DS/TA2P400/TA3J6500 and an OEM QJ2 lug table. No OEM supersession tables were found. Distributor web fetches were limited (superbreakers 429). Plug-in bases, mounting screw kits and VL lugs were deferred (see leads).
