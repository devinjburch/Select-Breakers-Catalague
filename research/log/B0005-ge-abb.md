APN investigation: 'APN' in GE nomenclature = Spectra plug-in panelboard interior type (APNB = bolt-on), per BuyLog p.11-37/11-28/11-47; APN48/APN84/APN126 (+AD suffix) = A-Series permanent circuit number kits; APN49/APN99 = Spectra permanent circuit number tabs. A-Series power panels are AQP/AEP/ADP, not APN. Not found: OEM docs for NLAB/NHB/CCB-era legacy panels, CDP, AV-Line, ReliaGear LV; ReliaGear neXT kit catalog number. Footnote digits fused to catalog numbers in the PDF text were stripped (e.g. 'MB23325' -> MB2332 with footnote 5, 'AMC6FJFP1' -> AMC6FJFP) and noted in claims. AMC/AMCB base numbers without 'FP' recorded as alias (conf B, from nomenclature). DEH44151 (TNK400 neutral kit for safety switches) seen but out of scope.
