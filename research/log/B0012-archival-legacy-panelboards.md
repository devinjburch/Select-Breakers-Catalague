## Narrative

Method: (1) archive.org full-text search via services/search/beta/page_production (service_backend=fts) for designations (NLAB, NQOB, NHB, NLTQ, Converti-Fuse, AV-Line, I-T-E, Mullenbach, NEHB, ML-1); downloaded open djvu text for ECM 1946-09, 1955-03, 1957-06, 1960-05, 1960-09, 1962-05 and the 1951 GE Supply 'Wiring Materials' catalog; access-restricted items (Electrozad 1985, GE GEJ-3006A, textbooks) were read only through item-scoped FTS highlight snippets ('identifier:<id> AND <term>'). (2) Eaton Volume 12 CA08100014E (Mars Electric mirror) Tab 4 extracted with pdftotext: Cutler-Hammer vintage types MP40, MP100, MP2/MP-200, PB, PH/PH-L, EE, EP, CHP/CHB, NFB with ratings, layouts and replacement charts. (3) Eaton Bid Manager help page for MP40/MP100/CDP/FDP kit rules. (4) Relectric mounting-hardware catalogue (42 listing pages, 981 items) parsed and ~70 detail pages read for spec blocks; Superbreakers Shopify JSON for MP40 kit text. (5) Schneider FAQs FA130530, FA233845, FA96717 read; the rest returned 403. (6) Found and mined Square D Digest 152 (1970), an open archive.org scan: panelboard index p.55 (NQO, NQH, NQO-LX, NQOB, NQHB, NQOB-LX, NA1B, NA1B-LX, NH1B, NH1B-LX, HCN, HCM, HCW, HCWM, NTFB, NTHB, QMB), type pages (NA1B p.66, NH1B p.68, I-Line pp.72-73, QMB pp.80-83), accessories p.84 and ordering/replacement p.85 - about 60 OEM part numbers (ground bar kits, padlock attachments, sub-feed lugs, I-Line neutrals/blanks/extensions, QMB blanks/neutrals/adaptors/extension assemblies). (7) Trademark register 1976 snippet: POW-R-LINE registered 1975.
Failures: RP01400003E and PL01400003E not obtainable (eaton.com errors, Cloudflare, 403, mirror captcha, web.archive.org unreachable). se.com FAQs blocked after 3 reads. HathiTrust and Google Books not attempted in depth due to time.
Key findings: Cutler-Hammer pre-1994 panelboards are MP40 (CB distribution, to 1600 A, successor PRL4B), MP100 (fusible, M50 switches, successor PRL4F), MP2/MP-200 group-mounted switchboard, PB (CH/CHB, 400 A, successor PRL1a, no connectors), PH (800 A MLO / 600 A MB) and PH-L (225 A) (successor PRL3a), EE and EP plug-in power panelboards (1200 A, successor PRL5P), CHP/CHB lighting and NFB; Cutler-Hammer also sold CDP, NLAB, NPLAB, NA1B, NH1B, NDP, HNDP built on Westinghouse breakers under the 1960s supply agreement (Mullenbach purchase 1962). 'Pow-R-Line' itself dates from the 1988 Westinghouse redesign. NLAB was used by both Westinghouse (Quicklag) and GE (TQ/TQC; 'formerly NTQ'), and by ITE-Siemens Canada (BQ) - designation alone does not identify the maker. GE 1955 list: lighting NAB, NHB, NLAB, NLTQ, NLTQX, NTP, NTC; power NCB, CCB, Swing-Wa, Converti-Fuse. GE GEG-13610B gives ~20 OEM renewal part numbers for DB/NLTQ/NLAB Style 5. Square D NQO/NQOB were advertised in 1960 and catalogued in 1970 (conflicts with a 'late 1960s-1987' statement); Square D also had an NH1B (480 V, FY/FA breakers) in 1970 - NEHB is absent from the 1970 index, so NEHB came later (obsoleted 1995 per FAQ summary); MLN/ML 1962-1968 superseded by I-Line, with an 'MLW' panel still serviced in 1970; I-Line HCN (26 in), HCM (32 in), HCW (41 in), HCWM existed by 1970.
Conflicts flagged: MP403XBLANK dealer spec says PRL3A; SG6DPK/SG6SPK titled 'AV' but spec says APNB-APN; 5JJ2 title NAB/NHB vs spec FC20/VB; MP40KK400-3 breaker list differs between dealers; THJV6DPK twin vs single.
Not found: GE 'ADS', early AQ dates, GE 'Q-Line'; ITE 'Type HE' panel, 'ITE CDP' original construction; Square D HCW introduction date (exists by 1970); Cutler-Hammer 'Type MS' switchboard.
