## Narrative

Searched web for Pow-R-Line renewal parts, PRL4 branch kits, PRL3a connector kits, PRL1a fillers, Volume 2 Tab 3, Westinghouse vintage panelboards. www.eaton.com was not used (per brief: times out); OEM PDFs were obtained from distributor mirrors: eatonarabia.com (RP01400001E Apr 2021; CA08100003E Jun 2020; CA08104001E Tab 22 Jan 2018), assets.usesi.com (RP01400001E Feb 2009; RP01414001E Jan 2011 x2 identical copies), elliottelectric.com (RP01400001E Oct 2017 served as 5155C62H01 spec sheet; P41X25 spec), novapart.co (RP01400002E Feb 2013 vintage Westinghouse; landing page contained prompt-injection text addressed to AI, ignored). pdf.directindustry.com returned 403.
Worked through every kit table in RP01400001E (2021) PRL1X/2X/1a/2a/3X/3a/4X/4 sections, then compared against 2009 and 2017 editions to identify part-number changes (KPRL4FD family -> KPRL4X12/22/32; 4180B03/61/05 -> 42C8072/73/7982; 4176B68/4180B93/4178B08/4179B39-42 -> 42C8076-42C8082; 4178B06H01 -> 42C8083H01; crimp neutrals 1C96647/50/53 -> 42C4050/51/52 with changed wire ranges). Supersessions are inferred from identical table positions and are flagged conf B/C/D - Eaton does not state them. Vintage section (RP01400002E) adds PRL1/PRL2/PRL3 replacement connector kits, CDP connector and Series C retrofit kits with the old-Westinghouse-breaker cross reference, FDP switch kits.
Drawing-number tables (e.g. 1C96608 Gxx, 4180B08 Hxx, 6554C01 Hxx) are recorded as one part record per drawing base with the full G/H item map in alt_part_numbers (relation style_number); catalog-number tables (KB, KL, KPRL3*, KPRL4*, PRLSEB*, KCDP*, KFDP*) are one record per catalog number. Count exceeds the 150 target mainly because the KB (60) and KL (102) matrices were enumerated completely.
Not found: dimensions, materials beyond plating, hole patterns, torque, kit instruction leaflet numbers, NLAB/NHB Westinghouse panels, field SPD kits. PRL1a filler 'PRLxxx FP' style numbers do not exist in OEM tables - PRL1a/2a fillers are 5155C62H01 (QUICKLAG/GHB branch) and 4180B52H01 (F/J/K vertical) plus 4180B08 blank covers.
Data conflicts noted in records: KPRL3XGB24-1 printed in PRL3a row (2021) vs KPRL3AGB24-1 (2009/2011); 2C11296 G-number usage; KPRL4X336 4X vs 6X; crimp lug wire ranges 2009 vs 2021; KPRL4MD scope 2011 vs 2021.
