B0015 Square D legacy + unmined documents.

4) Digest 152 (1970): djvu OCR is word-fragmented, so page images were fetched (archive.org/download/squareddigest152/page/n<leaf>.jpg) and read visually: NQO/NQOB fronts NQC/MSC/MDC/LX with box dims (pp.58-59), I-Line 1970 HC boxes (-B/-DB) and fronts (-C, -T, HCS-..-T, -TS) for HCN/HCM/HCW/HCWM interiors (pp.72-73), QMB QM-31xx-B boxes and QM-31xx-TS fronts (p.78).
Not done: Supplemental & Obsolescence Digest itself (not found as PDF); NEHB kit tables (NEHB parts 'not available' per FAQ summary); NH1B/NA1B/ML specific box/front pages of Digest 152; 80043-831-01 and 80043-401-03 bulletins.
