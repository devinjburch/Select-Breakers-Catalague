## Narrative

Searched for OEM catalog tables. Eaton.com and se.com/download.schneider-electric.com blocked (empty reply / 403 / 503) so used distributor-hosted OEM PDFs: Eaton CA08101001E Nov 2008 full Tab 12 (Kempston mirror) for all Series C/G terminal and external accessory tables; CA08101001E May 2008 J-frame excerpt (Rexel); CA08100005E Oct 2015 excerpt pages (Warshauers) and April 2014 EG-frame pages (CMA/Flodyne); Square D 0611CT1001 R01/13 (RS mirror) Section 9 Tables 118-130; Square D Digest Section 3 2012 (Farnell) and 2016 (Kendall) mechanical/compression/PDC/control-wire lug tables. KM Parts reseller pages used to cross-check newer Eaton tables (conf C). Notable conflicts recorded as questions: Eaton interphase barrier numbering (2008: J,K=IPB3, L,M=IPB4; newer per KM: J,K=IPB2, L=IPB3, M=IPB4); J-frame shield 6641C16G0x load vs line end; TA350K 350 vs 400 A; 3TA150EF metric range; Square D Table 121 CU L-frame lug rows column-scrambled in extraction; PDC3JD20 vs PDC3JD2. Eaton web spec sheets label F-frame terminals 'Frame J-K' (taxonomy error). Not found: Square D MA/MH and PowerPact M/P/R untapped lug OEM tables (two distributor-only C records added), E-frame/FY/QB-QJ lug kits, KA/KH-era Digest tables, any explicit OEM supersession statements for lug kits. Panelboard connecting straps and rear studs flagged NOT UL listed by OEM. Torque values mostly unknown (only PowerPact terminal nut 80-90 lb-in A; TA250KB 275 lb-in C).
